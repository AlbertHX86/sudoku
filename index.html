    Research: Sudoku and the prefrontal cortex

    In Sudoku every row, column and 3×3 box must hold 1–9 exactly once. Solving one means keeping candidates in working memory, applying rules and checking your choices, which are jobs the prefrontal cortex is central to [1].

    What studies saw during the puzzle

    • fNIRS in 28 young adults: oxygenated haemoglobin rose at all 16 prefrontal recording sites while they filled 3×3 boxes and an easy 9×9 puzzle. Medial prefrontal areas responded more once row and column rules came into play [2].
    • A 2026 systematic review of six imaging studies (five fMRI, one fNIRS): Sudoku engaged frontoparietal executive-control regions, including the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, and quietened the default-mode network, more so for harder puzzles [3].
    • In 19,078 adults aged 50–93, those who did number puzzles more often scored better on tests of attention, working memory and reasoning [4].

    What this does not show

    The imaging studies are few, small and mostly single-session, so they show what the brain does while you play, not that playing changes it [3]. The puzzle-frequency study is correlational: people with sharper minds may simply enjoy puzzles more [4]. Brain training reliably improves the trained task, but evidence that it transfers to other tasks or daily life is weak [5]. Enjoy Sudoku as a puzzle, not a treatment.

    数独要求每一行、每一列、每个 3×3 宫都恰好包含 1–9。解题需要在工作记忆中保留候选数字、运用规则并检查自己的选择，而这些正是前额叶皮层的核心功能 [1]。

    研究在解题过程中观察到了什么

    • 对 28 名青年人的 fNIRS 研究：在填写 3×3 宫和一道简单的 9×9 数独时，前额叶全部 16 个记录点的氧合血红蛋白都升高；引入行、列规则后，内侧前额叶的反应更强 [2]。
    • 2026 年一篇纳入 6 项脑成像研究（5 项 fMRI、1 项 fNIRS）的系统综述：数独会激活包括背外侧前额叶在内的额顶执行控制区域，并抑制默认模式网络，题目越难抑制越明显 [3]。
    • 在 19,078 名 50–93 岁的成年人中，越常做数字谜题的人，在注意力、工作记忆和推理测试中得分越高 [4]。

    这些研究不能说明什么

    脑成像研究数量少、样本小，而且大多只测一次，只能说明玩的时候大脑在做什么，不能说明玩数独会改变大脑 [3]。谜题频率研究是相关性研究：也可能是头脑更敏锐的人更喜欢做谜题 [4]。脑力训练能可靠地提高被训练任务本身的成绩，但迁移到其他任务或日常生活的证据很弱 [5]。把数独当作一项益智游戏来享受，而不是治疗手段。
